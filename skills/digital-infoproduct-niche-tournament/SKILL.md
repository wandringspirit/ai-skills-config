# Digital Info-Product Niche Tournament & Selection Playbook (`skills/digital-infoproduct-niche-tournament`)

A battle-tested framework for identifying, debating, stress-testing, and executing high-margin digital info-products (PDF playbooks, templates, checklists, SOPs, micro-courses, and digital toolkits) specifically engineered for high purchasing power markets (US and UK).

---

## 1. US & UK Digital Buyer Economics & Market Dynamics

To succeed selling digital products to US and UK audiences without high churn or fatal ad CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost):

### A. The 3 Purchasing Power Archetypes
1. **The Corporate Expenser (B2B / Career / Operations)**:
   - Mindset: "I can swipe the company card or write this off on my taxes. If it saves 2 hours or protects my job, $97–$297 is an instant no-brainer."
   - Target price points: $97 (Tripwire/Standard), $197–$497 (Executive / Team License).
   - Refund rate: < 1.5%. Extremely low support overhead.
2. **The High-Intent Solopreneur / Operator (Wealth / ROI)**:
   - Mindset: "If I spend $37 on this template/playbook and it gets me one client or automates one process, ROI is 10x immediately."
   - Target price points: $27–$47 (Front-end impulse), $97–$147 (Full system upsell).
   - Refund rate: 2–4%. Requires high immediate clarity and plug-and-play assets.
3. **The Urgent Pain Consumer (Health / High-Stress Life Milestones)**:
   - Mindset: "I am sleep-deprived, anxious, or overwhelmed right now at 2 AM. I need a clear step-by-step system, not a 300-page textbook."
   - Target price points: $19–$39 (Impulse buy), $67 (Full protocol bundle).
   - Refund rate: 3–6%. Requires immediate psychological reassurance and day-1 actionable wins.

### B. Why Generic "Vitamins" Fail in the US/UK Market
- **The "Vitamin" Trap**: Generic productivity, general mindfulness, generic "Learn Python", general fitness tips. Free on YouTube, ChatGPT can generate it in 5 seconds, Meta CPMs ($25–$50) will eat 100% of the margin.
- **The "Aspirin" Law**: Solve a bleeding-neck, highly specific, high-cost problem with proprietary workflows, exact formulas, templates, or compliance-checked steps that cannot be hallucinated by generic AI prompts.

### C. The 5 Evaluation Criteria (The "P.R.I.C.E." Scorecard)
Every niche candidate must be graded on a 1–10 scale across:
1. **Pain & Urgency (P)**: How visceral is the problem? Is the buyer searching for a solution at 2 AM?
2. **Purchasing Power & Willingness to Pay (R - Resources)**: Does the target buyer in US/UK have high disposable income or corporate expense accounts?
3. **Implementation Velocity & Proof (I)**: Can the buyer achieve their first "Aha!" result within 30 minutes of opening the PDF?
4. **CAC & Organic Arbitrage (C)**: Can this sell profitably on Meta/TikTok ads or cleanly rank organically via SEO, communities, and affiliate word-of-mouth?
5. **AI Moat & Non-Commoditization (E - Exclusivity)**: Is the content proprietary, experience-backed, structured into ready-to-use assets (templates/checklists), and immune to zero-shot ChatGPT prompts?

## 2. The 10-Round Adversarial Tournament Protocol

When running multi-agent tournaments to debate and select the optimal niche:

- **Round 1: The Pitch & Core Value Proposition**: The Big Idea, specific target avatar, visceral pain point, and core deliverable format.
- **Round 2: US/UK Buyer Psychology & Pricing Power**: Price points ($27 to $297+), checkout impulse threshold, and willingness-to-pay dynamics.
- **Round 3: Acquisition, Ad Arbitrage & Traffic Feasibility**: CAC vs. LTV, Meta/TikTok hook angles, SEO/community distribution channels.
- **Round 4: Production Effort & Time-to-MVP**: Solopreneur build complexity, asset creation time (days vs weeks), format selection (Notion, PDF, Loom videos, Sheets).
- **Round 5: Refund & Chargeback Defense**: Preventing buyer remorse, handling Stripe dispute risks, delivering instant dopamine on page 1.
- **Round 6: The Cross-Examination (Adversarial Roast)**: Each contestant attacks the critical vulnerabilities and blindspots of the other contenders.
- **Round 7: Funnel Economics & Upsell Architecture**: Front-end impulse offer -> Order bump -> Core product upsell -> High-ticket or continuity backend.
- **Round 8: AI Disruption & Obsolescence Resilience**: Surviving LLM upgrades and ensuring long-term intellectual moat.
- **Round 9: The Quantitative Scoring Matrix**: Formal numerical evaluation across the 5 PRICE criteria to identify finalists.
- **Round 10: The Champion Blueprint & Execution Plan**: Crown the winning niche, provide the exact product title, core table of contents, target pricing, tech stack, and 14-day launch roadmap.

## 3. High-Converting PDF / Course Delivery Formats

1. **The "Execution Playbook" PDF (15–35 pages max)**:
   - Clean, professional typography (Daylight or Dark minimalist aesthetic).
   - No fluff, no introductory autobiographies.
   - 80% frameworks, step-by-step checklists, flowcharts, fill-in-the-blank scripts, and exact decision trees.
2. **The "Hybrid Notion / Sheet OS"**:
   - The PDF acts as the architectural manual.
   - Includes a 1-click duplicate Notion workspace, Google Sheet financial calculator, or Airtable pipeline.
   - Dramatically increases perceived value from $19 to $97+.
3. **The "Micro-Video Walkthrough" (Loom / Unlisted YouTube)**:
   - 3 to 5 bite-sized videos (5–10 mins each) demonstrating how to use the templates in practice.

## 4. Tech Stack for Solopreneur US/UK Sales

- **Checkout & Digital Delivery**: Lemon Squeezy or Gumroad (acts as Merchant of Record, automatically handles US state sales tax and UK VAT MOSS).
- **Payment Gateways**: Stripe + Apple Pay + Google Pay (mandatory for >60% mobile US/UK checkout conversion).
- **Landing Page / Funnel**: Framer, Carrd, or Webflow (minimalist, hyper-fast, mobile-first).
- **Email Delivery & Sequences**: ConvertKit (Kit), Postmark, or Cloudflare Email Routing + Resend.
